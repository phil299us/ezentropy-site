About ezentropy LLC

We build simple, useful software for families in the places they live.

ezentropy LLC is a Maryland limited liability company headquartered in Baltimore. The company develops, publishes and operates DMVKids on the Apple App Store, on Google Play and on the web at dmvkids.app.

Finding things to do with kids usually means checking a dozen library calendars, parks department pages and museum sites. Our goal is to do that work once, every day, and give families one clear list they can trust.

How we work

  • Respect for families' privacy. Browsing needs no account, we don't sell personal information, and our apps have no advertising.
  • Accurate, current listings. Events come from the organizations that run them, and every listing links back to its source.
  • Built for every phone. The same service on iPhone, Android and the web.

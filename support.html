Common questions

Is DMVKids free?

Yes. Browsing, searching, saving and reminders are all free.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and keeps your saved events and your kids' preferences across devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open More and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the data stored with it. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

An event is wrong or missing.

Use "Suggest an event" in the app's More tab, or email us a link to the event, and we'll fix it.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the app, open More → Notifications, or change them in your phone's Settings.

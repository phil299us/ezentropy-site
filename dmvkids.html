DMVKids

Skip the searching. Family fun across DC, Maryland and Virginia, in one free app.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play Use it on the web

What it does

One list, 70+ sources

Library storytimes, parks and recreation programs, museum days, nature center walks and festivals, collected every day.

Near you

Pick your home area and distance, and see what's on today, tomorrow and this weekend.

Search in plain words

Ask for things like "free indoor things for a 3-year-old this weekend".

Picks for your kids

Tell the app your kids' ages and what they love, and For You shows events that match.

Adventures

A map of playgrounds, parks, trails and outdoor places to go any day.

Save and remember

Save events, add them to your calendar, and get a reminder before they start.

Free for families

DMVKids is free to use, with no ads. Families don't need an account to browse. Signing in is optional and keeps saved events and picks across devices.

For organizers

Run kids' events in the DMV? Tell us about them at theteam@ezentropy.com, or use "Suggest an event" in the app.
